          How to Play

          A secret whole number is chosen at random. Type a guess and press Enter. You'll be told if it's too low ⬆️ or too high ⬇️.

          The timer starts on your first guess. Invalid, out-of-range or repeated guesses never cost an attempt.

          Classic: one round, limited guesses and time.
          Time Attack: one shared clock; every correct guess adds bonus seconds.
          Endless: rounds get harder until you fail one.

          Each round has 1 free hint; earn more from daily rewards and achievements. Hints cost points but never reveal the number.
